# AETHER-LOGOS Hedge Market Audit

## Overview
The Risk Desk operates as a **Parimutuel Prediction Market** built on Solana via the `prediction-market` Anchor program. It allows buyers to hedge logistics risks (e.g., customs delays, transit exceptions) by staking USDC against a specific "shipment digital twin" (a compressed NFT tracking the shipment).

## Hedge Mechanics & Pool Logic
1. **Staking (Placing a Hedge)**
   - Users can stake USDC on the **Yes** or **No** side of a logistics outcome.
   - When a user places a hedge via `place_hedge`, their USDC is transferred directly from their wallet to a secure PDA token vault (`market_vault`).
   - The user's stake is recorded in a `HedgePosition` PDA, which tracks their chosen `side` and total `amount`.

2. **Parimutuel Pool Pricing**
   - The market does not use fixed odds or an Automated Market Maker (AMM). It uses a pure **parimutuel pool**.
   - The total payout is calculated based on the ratio of the total pool size vs. the total staked on the winning side.
   - For example, if there is $1,000 staked on Yes and $500 on No, a winning $100 bet on No would receive a proportional share of the entire $1,500 pool.

## How Funds Are Released
### 1. Market Resolution
To release funds, the market must first be transitioned from `Open` to `Resolved`.
- **Who Resolves:** According to `resolve_market`, the market can *only* be resolved by the **creator of the market** (the wallet that initialized the market PDA). For the hackathon scope, this is likely an admin key or an oracle bot controlled by you. 
- **The Check:** `require!(ctx.accounts.creator.key() == market.creator, MarketError::UnauthorizedResolver);`
- Once the creator sets the boolean `outcome` (true for Yes, false for No), the status becomes `Resolved`.

### 2. Claiming Winnings
Once the market is `Resolved`, anyone on the winning side can call `claim_winnings` to pull their USDC payout from the vault.
- **Who Claims:** The user themselves initiates the claim transaction. 
- **The Calculation:** The smart contract calculates the user's share using: `(User Stake * Total Pool) / Winning Side Total`.
- **The Release:** The `market_authority` PDA signs the SPL token transfer, releasing the proportional USDC from the `market_vault` directly into the user's `user_token_account`.

## Current Error Diagnosis: "Wallet not connected or market not loaded"
The error in your screenshot is a purely frontend UI safety net. The React component disables the "Place Hedge" button because either:
1. The Solana wallet isn't connected.
2. The specific `market_account` PDA hasn't been fetched from the blockchain yet (the UI is still loading the market data).
