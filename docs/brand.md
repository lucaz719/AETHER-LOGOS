## Gradients

Not used. This brand direction is intentionally flat and structural for enterprise trust and dense data readability.

## Usage guidance

1. Use token classes (`bg-background`, `bg-card`, `text-muted-foreground`, `border-border`) instead of hardcoded colors.
2. Reserve semantic green/red for market direction and decision actions only.
3. Keep visual weight in layout, spacing, and typography rather than decorative effects.
